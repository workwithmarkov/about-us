About

Built with care.
Grown with proof.

We are building an influencer marketing agency that earns its place one campaign at a time.

What we believe

The finest agencies are not the loudest. They are the ones whose brands, creators and results keep coming back.

Where we begin,
and where we are going.

We begin close to the work. We take on campaigns we can do properly, build real relationships with creators, and learn from every result.

As the relationships, the data and the operation grow, so does what we can offer. The aim is not to stay a standard agency. It is to build something much larger on a base of finest work.

Our name comes from the Markov chain, a way of describing how one state leads to the next. It is how we think about audiences, and about growing a business.

Principles

What we hold to.

Selection

Chosen, not crowded

A small number of right creators beats a large number of loud ones.

Evidence

Measured, not guessed

We weigh data first, then use judgement on top of it.

Delivery

Delivered, not promised

Agreed terms are the standard we hold ourselves and every creator to.
