Privacy

Last updated 24 October 2026

This page explains what information Markov's ("we", "us") handles when you use this website.

What this site collects

This site does not run accounts, analytics or advertising trackers, and it does not store what you type into its contact form. The form opens your own email app with a message addressed to us. Nothing is sent until you send it from your email app.

Information you send us

If you email us, we receive the details you include, such as your name, your brand or channel and your message. We use them to reply to you and to assess whether we can work together. We do not sell them.

Third-party services

  • Google Fonts supplies the typeface used on this site. Your browser requests it from Google, which can see your IP address and browser details.
  • Discord is linked from this site. If you follow the link, Discord's own terms and privacy policy apply.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete the emails you have sent us by writing to workwithmarkov@gmail.com.

Changes

If we change how this site works, we will update this page and its date.
